Contracts and warranties · German Experts, Abu Dhabi · 2024 to now

Service contracts, from price book to renewal

The group sells service contracts and warranties across several luxury and EV brands. I took over the CRM module that handles them and extended it, rather than starting again.

Problem
Contracts across several brands needed consistent pricing, renewals, payment options and documents.
What I built
A contract calculator, and extensions to the warranty and service contract module in the CRM.
Outcome
One flow from price book to quotation, contract and renewal, with PDF documents for the customer.

Context

Every brand prices its contracts differently, and a contract can come due by date or by mileage, whichever happens first.

The problem

  • Prices, renewal rules and payment options varied by brand and were hard to keep consistent.
  • Quotations needed limits and installment options applied the same way every time.

What I did

  • Built the contract calculator.
  • Extended the warranty and service contract module with per-brand price books, date and mileage renewals, validation rules, SQL functions, installment plans and PDF quotations and contracts.

How I approached it

  • Prices are data. Price books are uploaded per brand instead of being written into code.
  • Two renewal triggers. A contract comes due by date or by mileage.
  • Rules live in the quote. Validation rules and installment plans apply when the quotation is created, not after.
  • Extend, don't rewrite. The existing module kept working while it grew.

How it fits together

Where it landed

  • Pricing for each brand is managed through uploaded price books.
  • Renewals are triggered by date or by mileage.
  • Quotations and contracts are generated as PDFs.

Built with C# / .NET, SQL Server, SQL functions, CRM workflows and validation rules, PDF generation

Written at the level of what the system does. No company code, data, screenshots or internal details.
